### Agentic AI Defense: Decode-Time Provenance Binding for MCP

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31974
- Matched layers: market_context

arXiv:2610.31974v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Agentic AI systems using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) are vulnerable to indirect prompt injection through untrusted tool outputs. To counter this threat, we propose decode-time provenance binding, which combines HMAC-authenticated trust labels with an MCP-maintained provenance ledger and context-taint tracking.

### $\Psi$-0.6: Adaptive Streams of Thought in a Unified Vision-Language World Model

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32177
- Matched layers: ai_compute

We introduce $\Psi$-0.6, a unified vision-language world model that adaptively composes perception, language, geometry, and simulation within a shared reasoning process we call streams of thought. $\Psi$-0.6 offers a path toward adaptive physical reasoning -- not only predicting the physical world but also choosing the representations through which to reason.

- Source: arXiv CS.AI

To evaluate and improve long-horizon spatiotemporal reasoning, we introduce the LongVideo dataset and the 4DMemoryBench. We hope 4DMemory will facilitate the development of spatiotemporal agents capable of addressing complex, long-horizon tasks.

### A Foundation Model for Policy In-Context Longitudinal Causal Effect Estimation

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-10-27
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32863
- Matched layers: ai_compute

We introduce *Policy In-Context Longitudinal causal Effect estimation with Prior-data Fitted Networks* (**PICLE-PFN**), the first foundation model for longitudinal causal effect estimation that supports *dynamic* treatment policies. Pretrained over synthetic data sampled from longitudinal structural causal models, PICLE-PFN amortizes causal effect estimation across datasets, policies, and horizons.
