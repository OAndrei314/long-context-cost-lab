### A Fresh Look at Lamarckian Evolution and the Baldwin Effect

- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28703

In this work, we use modern empirical and theoretical methods to revisit Lamarckian and Baldwinian evolution and rigorously compare them with the generic Darwinian evolution.
